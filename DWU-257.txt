Feature: Align Price Ladder and Trader Order Indicators In Market Depth Widget

  As a trader
  I want the Escalator Market Depth widget to display price levels and trader order indicators consistently
  So that the ladder is easier to read and active orders are clearly associated with the correct side of the market

  Background:
    Given the user is logged into Darwin
    And the user navigates to the "Order Management" screen
    And the user opens an Escalator Market Depth widget for a selected instrument

  # Covers AC1, AC2, and AC6
  Scenario: Single centered price ladder layout structural alignment
    When the Escalator Market Depth widget renders the market data
    Then the sell price levels (pink) are displayed completely above the buy price levels (blue)
    And both sell and buy price values are horizontally aligned within a single, centralized price column
    And the layout structurally matches the approved Escalator Market Depth design

  # Covers AC3 and AC5
  Scenario: Display and positioning of trader order indicators for active Sell orders
    Given the user successfully submits a passive Sell order at a specific price level
    When the Escalator Market Depth widget updates to reflect the active order
    Then the trader order indicator appears exactly at the same price level row as the associated active sell order
    And the trader order indicator is explicitly positioned on the right side of the pink sell price cell

  # Covers AC4 and AC5
  Scenario: Display and positioning of trader order indicators for active Buy orders
    Given the user successfully submits a passive Buy order at a specific price level
    When the Escalator Market Depth widget updates to reflect the active order
    Then the trader order indicator appears exactly at the same price level row as the associated active buy order
    And the trader order indicator is explicitly positioned on the left side of the blue buy price cell
