  I want the quantity selection bar in the D2D Market Depth “Escalator” widget to be displayed vertically with predefined fixed quantities, so that I can quickly select commonly used sizes during fast market conditions.
  Background:
    Given that the user is authenticated in Darwin
    And navigates to the "Order Management" screen
