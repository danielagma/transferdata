Hi team, a process point before the QA round, so we all work from the same version of each ticket.

Over the last weeks several tickets changed after testing on them had started:
- DWU-550 had AC 6, AC 15 and the description edited in Jira.
- DWU-613's title and scope were extended twice, and its RFQ rule now lives only in DWU-730.
- DWU-418's InternalTradeType row was superseded by a comment, and DWU-417's TradeSrc row was replaced by DWU-613; both tables still show the old rules.
- DWU-549's "one id per event" rule exists only in a comment.
- A comment on DWU-432 was rewritten under its original date.

From now on I propose:
1. Once a ticket is In QA, its ACs are frozen. Any new rule or scope change goes into a new ticket, or is a written edit announced here with the reason.
2. Approved tickets are not edited; a follow-up ticket references them.
3. Every defect gets a ticket, even when the fix is on the STP Hub, TOMS or ref data side. The ticket says who owns it and when.
