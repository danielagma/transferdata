[Bug] STP Hub payload: Mifid ExecutionMktId and InvestmentMktId fields published as null on Tradeweb trades

**Overview**
On Tradeweb trades, Darwin publishes the Mifid short code fields to STP Hub as null, even though the trader has the codes set in Trader Platform Codes. User.TraderUuid is populated from the same screen.

* **Environment:** UAT2
* **Trade:** Tradeweb$20261002.SAN3.EUGV.274 (trader c0292415)

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Execute an RFQ on Tradeweb with a trader that has Trader Platform Codes set.
2. In OpenSearch, search: Event.Type: "StpHubPayloadSent" and "20261002.SAN3.EUGV.274"
3. Open Event.Document and check the Mifid block.

**Actual result:** "ExecutionMktId": null, "ExecutionMktIdType": null, "InvestmentMktId": null, "InvestmentMktIdType": null. User.TraderUuid is "5544".

**Expected result:** ExecutionMktId, ExecutionMktIdType, InvestmentMktId and InvestmentMktIdType are populated from the trader's codes in Trader Platform Codes.
