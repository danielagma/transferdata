# How to Launch a Trade with Allocations in Tradeweb

## Select an ISIN

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Paste the ISIN in the search box

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Press LookUp

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Select the instrument and press BUY or SELL

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Select the dealers

Select `SAN3` and `SANT`. Green is selected, red is not.

**The trade is received by `SAN3`.**

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Set the quantity and press Process Price

`Quantity (000)` is in thousands. `1,000` is a nominal of 1,000,000.

## Press Pre-Alloc

`#Accts` arrives at `1`. Press `Pre-Alloc` to split it.

## Press LOOKUP to add an account

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Select two accounts

Three accounts are available. You can select all three, but in this case select two.

Highlight the account, press `Select`, then repeat for the second one.

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Split the quantity between the two accounts and press SAVE

Fill the `Percent` column on each row. The two rows have to add up to `100.00`.

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**

## Press Send

> **[ SCREENSHOT ]**
